Source distribution
-------------------
The release must provide the application source archive and the companion Qt
and vcpkg source archives produced by packaging/New-SourceBundle.ps1 alongside
the binary ZIP. Keep the files in the same download location and accessible to
the same recipients. Qt source includes its build scripts and licence files;
BUILD-INFO retains the configuration of the prebuilt Qt used for this package.
Source URLs in notices are supplementary, not a replacement for these archives.
